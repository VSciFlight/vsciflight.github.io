Services

What automation services I build

Business process automation

The repetitive middle of a process gets automated. The judgement calls stay with your team.

  • Mapping how the process really runs today, including the undocumented parts
  • Scheduled jobs that replace the manual daily and weekly routine
  • Approval steps routed to a person only when the rules say so

Data pipelines & ETL

Data moves between your systems on schedule, without anyone exporting anything.

  • Extract, validate, transform and load between databases, sheets and SaaS tools
  • Recurring reports built overnight and delivered before you start work
  • Health checks that tell you a job failed, instead of you finding out later

API development & integration

New APIs, or the connective tissue between tools that were never meant to talk.

  • REST APIs built from scratch, with authentication and documentation
  • Integrations across CRM, ERP, billing, storage and spreadsheets
  • Webhook handlers, retries and queues so nothing is silently dropped

Web & data scraping

Collection pipelines for the data you currently gather by hand.

  • Prices, listings, competitors, public records or your own portals
  • Built to survive layout changes, rate limits and logins
  • Clean exports to your database, sheet or dashboard on a schedule

Telegram & chat bots

Internal tools that live where your team already talks, instead of behind another login.

  • Alerts and daily digests pushed to the right group or person

Python development

General development work, including rescuing what someone built before me.

Which manual processes are worth automating

TodayAfter

Someone re-types order details from email into the ERP every morning.

Emails are parsed and written in automatically. Only exceptions reach a person.

The Monday report takes half a day of exporting and pasting.

It builds overnight and is waiting in the inbox at 07:00.

Two systems hold the same customer data and quietly disagree.

One sync job, one source of truth, and a daily list of what drifted.

Competitor prices get checked by hand, when someone remembers.

A scraper checks on schedule and pings you when something moves.

A job failed on Thursday. You found out on Monday.

Every pipeline reports its own health. A failure is a message, not a discovery.

  1. Call

    You walk me through the process that hurts. I tell you honestly whether automating it is worth it.

  2. Map & quote
